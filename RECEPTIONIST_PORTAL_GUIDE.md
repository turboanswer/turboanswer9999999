## 3. Help Assistant — "Got a hard question?"

Use this when you're not sure what to do, or when a customer has a tricky problem.

**How to use it:**
1. Click the **Help Assistant** tab.
2. Type the customer's problem OR a technical question in plain English.
   - Example (customer issue): *"Customer was charged twice and wants a refund."*
   - Example (technical): *"Why would the AI suddenly stop answering?"*
3. Click **Find the procedure** (or press the button) and wait a moment.

**What you get back:**
- **Guidance** — a short, clear answer telling you what to do.
- **Matching procedures** — the exact step-by-step guides that fit the problem. Click any one to open the full steps.
- For pure technical questions, it may give an answer with no procedure attached — that's normal.

The assistant knows the whole TurboAnswer system (billing, accounts, the app, the AI, security) and is powered by Azure.

---

## 4. Accounts — manage a customer

Use this when a customer contacts you and you need to act on their account.

**Find a customer:**
1. Click the **Accounts** tab.
2. Type the customer's **name or email** in the search box.
3. Click the customer in the list.

**What you can do for that customer:**
- **Change their plan** — switch their subscription tier (Free, Pro, Research, Enterprise).
- **Cancel their subscription** — stops their paid plan (and cancels billing through PayPal).
- **View their chats / issues** — see what the customer has recently been chatting about, so you understand their problem before helping.
- **Escalate to engineering** — if it's something you can't fix, fill in the escalation box and send it. This emails the engineering team at **support@turboanswer.it.com** with the customer's details.

> Always look at "View chats / issues" first — it tells you the real problem in the customer's own words.

---

## 5. Procedures — the how-to library

Use this when you already know the topic and want the exact steps.

**How to use it:**
1. Click the **Procedures** tab.
2. **Search** by typing keywords (e.g. "refund", "password", "android crash").
3. Narrow down with the **Category** and **Difficulty** filters if needed.
4. Click any procedure to open its full step-by-step instructions.

There are **1,000 procedures** covering everything from easy questions to complex problems (billing, login, the mobile app, voice assistant, refunds, and more).

---

## 6. Everyday workflow (quick version)

1. Customer contacts you.
2. Go to **Accounts** → search the customer → **View their chats** to understand the issue.
3. Not sure what to do? Go to **Help Assistant**, describe the problem, follow the guidance.
4. Need exact steps? Open the matching **Procedure**.
5. Need to change or cancel their plan? Do it from the customer's **Accounts** page.
6. Can't solve it? Use **Escalate to engineering** from the customer's Accounts page.
